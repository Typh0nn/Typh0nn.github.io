Japan Tobacco International · Master Data Manager

C-SKU: giving every JTI platform one definition of a product

Problem
Product identity differed by system. What finance called a product, supply chain and commercial reporting each called something slightly different — so figures could not be joined without a manual mapping layer that broke whenever the portfolio changed.
Approach
Introduced the C-SKU (Commercial Stock Keeping Unit) as the product definition standard and rolled it out across all JTI data platforms. Owned global product definition standards, ran product attribute management, optimised the data mapping processes and put KPI-driven validation in front of the data rather than behind it. Connected product development in SAP ERP to financial processes in SAP S/4HANA, feeding analytics in SAP Analytics Cloud.
Result
Seamless data integration across platforms, and both local and global reporting from a single product definition. This is what made the 70+ market reporting programme possible at all.

Coty Inc. · Customer Service Master Data Manager

Four markets, four ways of working — reduced to one

Problem
The regional Customer Service master data function spanned Iberia, the Canary Islands, Benelux and Italy, each with its own local practice. Operational silos meant no shared view of what the team actually did, and no shared standard for the data it produced.
Approach
Led harmonisation of regional workflows and conducted advanced process mapping and standardisation of over 300 services provided by the team. Established a unified single source of truth for master data across every in-scope market, and moved the team from manual processing toward automated, data-driven workflows — including technical upskilling in SQL and Power BI.
Result
Materially higher operational transparency and service consistency, and a team structured around one standard rather than four. Reported directly to the VP Supply Chain Europe.

Coty Inc. · Migration readiness

Automated validation so a SAP → Databricks migration had nothing to trip over

Problem
Migrating master data out of SAP into Databricks fails on the records nobody checked. Manual pre-migration review across Benelux and Southern Europe would not have been repeatable, and would not have survived the second dry run.
Approach
Implemented automated data validation scripts for the BNL and Southern Europe markets, run against the actual migration scope rather than a sample. Partnered with IT on the cross-functional data connections in Databricks that the target model depended on.
Result
100% data readiness confirmed before the migration, on a repeatable check rather than a one-off review.
