Delivery case studies
Japan Tobacco International · Global Harmonized Reporting Project

One reporting standard for 70+ markets, replacing a patchwork of local Excel

Problem
Markets reported financial, retail-audit and consumer data in their own formats. HQ could not compare them without manual rework, and every market maintained its own Excel chain.
Approach
Two-year programme under HQ Strategic Insights supervision. Built Power BI composite models over a centralised Azure SQL data warehouse, with market data (shipments, distributor, sell-in/sell-out via Azure Analysis Services), consumer insight and financial actuals and planning connected into one semantic layer. Row-level security so each market sees its own scope.
Result
450+ report views per day, actively used by 300+ stakeholders across Western and Eastern Europe, the Philippines and Geneva HQ. Local and global reporting from the same definitions for the first time.

Japan Tobacco International · Reporting automation

20+ manual Excel processes retired — 3 FTE of effort recovered

Problem
Recurring reporting was assembled by hand every cycle: copy, paste, reconcile, re-send. Slow, error-prone, and impossible to audit after the fact.
Approach
Traced each process back to its source, moved the logic into the warehouse and the model, and replaced the manual step with a refreshing Power BI page. Where a process could not be modelled away, it was standardised first, then automated.
Result
Over 20 processes automated, delivering efficiency gains equivalent to 3 FTE saved, and a reporting layer the team could extend rather than rebuild.
